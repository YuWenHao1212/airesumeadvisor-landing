## 1. Product Overview

### What is AI Resume Advisor?

AI-powered resume optimization platform that helps job seekers create targeted resumes matching specific job postings.

### Core Value Proposition

- Analyze job descriptions to extract keywords
- Compare LinkedIn profile against job requirements
- Generate ATS-optimized, tailored resumes
- Recommend courses to fill skill gaps

### Target Users

- Job seekers (actively looking for new positions)
- Career changers
- Recent graduates
- Professionals seeking promotions

### Pricing Model

| Free        | $0    | 10 credits/day | All features, limited usage |
| Daily Pass  | $10   | 1 day       | Unlimited access        |

## 3. Issues to Address

### Copy Quality Issues

1. **Duplicate titles**: Tab 2 and Tab 3 have same title "Upskill Smarter: Personalized Growth Recommendations"
2. **Generic language**: Many phrases feel AI-generated and lack uniqueness
3. **Weak value proposition**: Benefits not clearly quantified
4. **Missing urgency**: No time-sensitive elements
5. **Social proof lacks specifics**: "Thousands" is vague, no specific numbers

### Conversion Optimization

1. **CTA clarity**: Multiple "Get Started" links could be consolidated
2. **Pricing justification**: Why Daily $10 vs Weekly $20?
3. **Trust signals**: Need more social proof (testimonials, stats)
4. **Risk reversal**: Missing money-back guarantee or trial info

### Target Audience Alignment

- Current copy is generic for "job seekers"
- Could be more specific to pain points:
  - "Tired of applying to 50 jobs with no response?"
  - "Spending hours customizing resumes for each application?"

## 4. Optimization Goals

### Primary Goals

1. **Increase conversion rate** from visitor to signup
2. **Improve clarity** of value proposition
3. **Strengthen trust** with specific social proof
4. **Create urgency** without being pushy

### Specific Improvements

1. **Hero Section**
   - Make headline more specific and benefit-driven
   - Add specific number (e.g., "83% match rate")
   - Consider problem-agitation-solution structure

2. **Features Section**
   - Fix duplicate titles
   - Make benefits more concrete with numbers
   - Add user success metrics

3. **Social Proof**
   - Add specific statistics
   - Include real testimonial quotes
   - Show company logos where users work

4. **Pricing**
   - Better explain value of each tier
   - Highlight savings of weekly vs daily
   - Add comparison to competitors

5. **Bottom CTA**
   - Create more urgency
   - Reiterate key benefit
   - Consider adding limited-time offer

## 5. Copywriting Frameworks to Consider

### PAS (Problem-Agitation-Solution)
1. **Problem**: Job applications getting ignored
2. **Agitation**: Hours spent on each resume, no results
3. **Solution**: AI-powered customization in minutes

### AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action)
1. **Attention**: Shocking statistic about job applications
2. **Interest**: How AI changes the game
3. **Desire**: Success stories and results
4. **Action**: Clear, compelling CTA

### Before-After-Bridge
1. **Before**: Struggling with generic resumes
2. **After**: Landing dream job with tailored applications
3. **Bridge**: Our AI Resume Builder

## 6. Questions for New Conversation

1. **Target User Persona**
   - Who is the ideal customer?
   - What is their biggest pain point?
   - What have they tried before?

2. **Unique Selling Proposition**
   - What makes AI Resume Advisor different?
   - Why should users choose this over competitors?

3. **Success Metrics**
   - What numbers can we share? (users, success rate, time saved)
   - Any specific testimonials or case studies?

4. **Brand Voice**
   - Professional and authoritative?
   - Friendly and supportive?
   - Urgent and action-oriented?

5. **Key Messages**
   - What 3 things should every visitor remember?
   - What objections need to be overcome?
