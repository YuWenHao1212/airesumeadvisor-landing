## Issues Encountered & Solutions

### 1. Windows App Service Plan doesn't support Node.js

**Error**: `Windows runtime 'NODE|18-lts' is not supported`

**Solution**: Deleted plan and recreated with `--is-linux` flag

### 2. ESLint v9 requires flat config

**Error**: `ESLint couldn't find an eslint.config.(js|mjs|cjs) file`

**Solution**: Created `eslint.config.js` with flat config format, installed `@eslint/js`

### 3. www subdomain needs separate TXT verification

**Error**: `A TXT record pointing from asuid.www.airesumeadvisor.com... was not found`

**Solution**: Added TXT record `asuid.www` with Azure verification ID

### 4. Bubble requires A records, not CNAME

**Error**: Bubble couldn't validate CNAME record

**Solution**: Changed from CNAME to 4 A records pointing to Bubble IPs

### 5. DNS caching during propagation

**Issue**: Google DNS returned old CNAME while other servers returned new A records

**Solution**: Waited for DNS cache to expire (~30 minutes)
