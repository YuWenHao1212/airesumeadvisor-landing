## Technical Decisions

### Why Lossless WebP?

| Option | File Size | Quality | Decision |
|--------|-----------|---------|----------|
| q85 | Small (~57KB) | Noticeable blur | ❌ Rejected |
| q95 | Medium (~90KB) | Slight softness | ❌ Rejected |
| Lossless | Large (~347KB) | Perfect | ✅ Chosen |

**Rationale**: Product screenshots need to be sharp and readable. The slight increase in load time is acceptable for better visual quality.

### Why Remove External Frame?

User-provided screenshots already included MacBook mockup styling. Adding another frame created visual noise and redundancy.
