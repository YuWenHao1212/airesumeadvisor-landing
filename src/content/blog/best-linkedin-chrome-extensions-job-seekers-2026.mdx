LinkedIn is where jobs get found. But let's be honest—the native experience isn't great for job seekers.

You can't easily tell if you're qualified for a role before applying. You can't track which jobs you've already applied to. You can't filter out positions that need 10 years of experience when you only have 3.

That's where Chrome extensions come in.

I've spent weeks testing dozens of LinkedIn extensions. Most are built for recruiters and salespeople. But some are genuinely useful for job seekers.

Here are the ones worth installing.

---

## How I Categorized These Extensions

LinkedIn extensions for job seekers fall into three categories:

1. **Job-Fit Analysis** — Check if you're qualified before applying
2. **Job Tracking & Autofill** — Track applications and speed up form filling
3. **LinkedIn Enhancements** — Add features LinkedIn doesn't provide natively

I'll cover the best tools in each category.

## Job Tracking & Autofill

### Huntr

**What it does:** A Kanban-style job tracker that lets you save jobs from any site—aggregators like LinkedIn and Indeed, boards like Dice, and ATS systems like Greenhouse and Lever. Also auto-fills job applications in one click.

**Why it's useful:** If you're applying to multiple jobs, you need a system. Huntr replaces messy spreadsheets with a visual board where you can track applications from "Wishlist" to "Applied" to "Interview" to "Offer." It saves job details (title, salary, location, company) automatically.

**Best feature:** One-click autofill that works on thousands of sites and top ATS systems—cuts application time to seconds.

**Pricing:** Free tier is generous. Paid plans add AI resume features.

**Rating:** 4.8/5 (250,000+ users, 5 million jobs tracked)

### Teal

**What it does:** Similar to Huntr—saves jobs, tracks applications, and provides resume keyword suggestions.

## LinkedIn Enhancements

### LinkedIn YOE Filter

**What it does:** Shows YOE badges on job cards (e.g., "YOE: 3-5 years") and visa sponsorship status. Lets you filter out positions requiring more experience than you have.

**Why it's useful:** LinkedIn doesn't let you filter by experience level. Perfect for new grads, entry-level professionals, and international candidates who want to skip mismatched positions.

**Pricing:** Free

**Rating:** 5.0/5 on Chrome Web Store

---

### LinkedIn Job Stats Viewer

**What it does:** Reveals hidden job posting statistics (total views, total applicants) directly on LinkedIn job pages. Updates automatically as you browse.

**Why it's useful:** Quickly assess competitiveness—seeing "500 applicants" might make you reconsider, while "10 applicants" could mean less competition.

**Pricing:** Free

**Rating:** 3.0/5 on Chrome Web Store

---

### LinkedIn Jobs Filter

**What it does:** Hides irrelevant job posts ("Applied," "Viewed," "We won't show you this job again") and filters by keywords (e.g., "Senior," "Developer"). Syncs settings across devices.

**Why it's useful:** Declutters your feed and shows only relevant opportunities. Supports all languages.

**Pricing:** Free

## What About Sales/Recruiter Extensions?

You might have seen extensions like Apollo.io, Lusha, or Kaspr recommended in "best LinkedIn extensions" lists. Those are built for sales teams and recruiters—they find contact info for outreach.

Unless you're doing cold outreach to hiring managers, you probably don't need them.
